How to play

  1. Drag a friend from your collection onto the playfield.
  2. Drop another friend on top of it to mix them.
  3. On a phone, tap two friends to mix (or press & hold to drag).
  4. Each day a Daily Friend appears on the playfield. Make it to grow your 🔥 streak (tap it for a clue)!
  5. Prefer a cozy evening? Settings has Day / Auto / Night look (Auto follows your clock).
  6. Earn cute Achievements badges as you play — open them from Settings (offline, no wallet)! Hearing Japanese names and acing the Name quiz earns badges too 🗾💮
  7. The little number on each friend shows how many new friends it can still help make. A ✓ means it's all mixed out!
  8. Open a friend from your collection and tap it to pet it: every friend has its own little voice and wiggle 💕
  9. While dragging, a tiny bubble peeks at the friend underneath: ? means new, 💭 tried means that pair didn't mix before, and a picture shows a mix you already know.
  10. Tap the sort pill next to search to line up your friends A–Z, Newest first, or by Most mixes left.
  11. Cleared the playfield by accident? Tap ↩ Undo right after (or press Ctrl/⌘+Z) to bring your friends back.
  12. Double-tap (or double-click) a friend on the playfield to make a twin, then mix the pair: some friends love meeting themselves! Keyboard: focus a friend and press D.
  13. In a friend's page, tap ‹ ›, swipe sideways or press the ← → arrow keys to flip through your found friends.
  14. In the Collection, tap 💖 Found, ❔ Missing or ★ Legendary to see just those friends.
  15. Playfield getting messy? Tap ✨ Tidy in its corner (or press T) and your friends hop into neat little rows, with twins side by side.
  16. Want a memory? With two or more friends on the playfield, tap 📸 Photo and they line up for a cute group photo you can save or share.
  17. Leave the playfield quiet for a little while and your friends get sleepy 💤. Tap anywhere to wake them up!
  18. Want a little tune? Turn on 🎵 Cozy music in Settings for a soft music-box lullaby. It plays slower at night, hushes when your friends doze off, and the mute button silences it too.
  19. Switching devices? Settings has a 💌 Friend Code: copy it on one device and paste it on the other to bring your friends along.
  20. Tap the 🤍 heart on a friend's page to make them a best friend 💗. Besties float to the top of your friends drawer, and the 💗 Besties chip in the Collection shows just them.
  21. Tap 📸 Save sticker on a friend's page to keep a cute picture card of them (on a phone you can share it straight to your photos or a chat).
  22. Now and then a mix comes out shiny ✨ with a rainbow glow and twinkles! Shiny friends stay shiny on the playfield, and the Collection keeps a ✨ on every friend you've met a shiny one of. Once you have one, a ✨ Shiny chip in the Collection shows just them.
  23. Open a friend in the Collection to see the day you met 📅. Old friends from before this update just say "An old friend" 💛.
  24. Find new friends back to back (within 30 seconds of the last one) to build a 🌟 Combo! The ring on the combo pill shows how much time is left, and the game remembers your best combo.
  25. Want to name a friend? Open it in your Collection and tap 🏷️ Give a nickname. Search finds friends by nickname too.
  26. On a friend's card, tap an ingredient or a Helped make 🐾 friend to hop straight to their card, and see how many friends are still hiding.
  27. Prefer things calm? Turn on 🫧 Gentle mode in Settings for fewer wiggles and sparkles and no phone buzzing.
  28. Friends too tiny (or too crowded)? Settings has 🔍 Friend size: Small, Comfy or Big, for the playfield and your friends drawer.
  29. Find more friends to unlock cute 🎨 Playfield wallpapers (clouds, candy stripes, hearts, stars and a rainbow). Pick one in Settings!
  30. Want the game on your phone? Settings has 📲 Add to Home Screen (when your browser supports it), so Element Friends opens in one tap and plays offline.
  31. Each autumn (Oct 15 to Nov 5) a spooky-cute 🎃 Pumpkin patch playfield pops up for everyone. Find it in Settings! In that season your friends on the playfield also dress up in tiny 🎃 costumes (switch them off in Settings).
  32. Leave your friends be for a moment and they 💬 chat: little speech bubbles pop up as they say hi to each other.
  33. Tap the little ⭐ star next to the game's name for a 🪩 dance party: every friend on the playfield bops along to a happy tune!
  34. On a phone or tablet, pull any sheet (like the Collection or Settings) 👇 down by its top to tuck it away. The little bar at the top shows where to grab.
  35. Playing with a keyboard? Press ? (or open ⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts in Settings) to see every key, like H for a hint, C for your Collection and / to search.
  36. Every friend's card shows its 🗾 Japanese name, and so does the "You discovered" pop-up. Tap it to hear how it sounds!
  37. Turn on あ Kana labels in Settings and your friends wear their names in kana, so you learn to read them as you play.
  38. Some friends bring their own weather 🌦️: put Rain, Snow, Sakura, Rainbow, Sun or Firefly on the playfield and watch the sky change. The newest one wins! Each weather has its own soft sound too (pitter-patter rain, tinkly snow); switch 🌦️ Weather sounds off in Settings if you like it quiet.
  39. Try the 🎌 Name quiz in Settings: pick the right Japanese name for five of your friends.
  40. Once a day, shake the 🎋 Omikuji box in Settings for a cute fortune slip, a lucky friend and a lucky element to try mixing.
  41. Been away a while? A friend pops up to 👋 wave hello when you come back (tap it to say hi back).
  42. Stuck? Press Hint for a clue. Can you find all the legendary friends?
